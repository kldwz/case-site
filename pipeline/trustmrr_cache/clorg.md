## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Renders any outfit from the user's own wardrobe onto their own body photo in one tap. No other app at this price point shows the user wearing the look.
- Problem solved: People cannot picture how the clothes they already own look together on their own body, so they default to the same few outfits, overbuy, and leave most of their wardrobe unworn.
- Pricing model: Free tier: 5 one-time credits. Weekly: $4.99(20 credits). Monthly: $9.99(60 credits). Yearly: $79.99(50 credits/month). Top-up: $2.99 for 10 credits.
- Target persona: Women aged 18 to 40, fashion enthusiasts, online clothing store
- Audience type: [Both](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=Both)
- Team size: 1 person
- Funding: Bootstrapped
- Estimated user count: 0
- Additional info: Live on Google Play. iOS build ready, pending Apple Developer enrollment. AI rendering via OpenAI GPT Image 2 at $0.032 per outfit. 108 style models for users who prefer not to upload a body photo. Net margins 78 to 85% on subscription revenue after platform commission.
- Markets: [Mobile Apps](https://trustmrr.com/category/mobile-apps), [AI](https://trustmrr.com/category/ai), [SaaS](https://trustmrr.com/category/saas)
