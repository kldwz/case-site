## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: BatchEdits is a bulk video editor for content creators that processes up to 50 videos simultaneously, automatically removing silence, adding captions in 50+ languages, and applying platform-specific cropping — reducing hours of manual editing to under 5 minutes.
- Problem solved: Editing is the wall that stops most creators from posting consistently and in volume. BatchEdits helps reduce the time-consuming process of editing multiple videos by automating silence removal, captioning, and formatting.
- Pricing model: Creator Starter: $20/month or $200/year (2 months free)
- Target persona: Content creators, YouTubers, podcasters, and agencies
