## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: 7Running transforms your training into a dynamic system, making you a better runner by converting your data into a personalized and evolving training plan.

- Pricing model: Free: diagnostic and allures, Club: €13/mo or €107/yr (-31%), Lifetime: €247 one-time.
