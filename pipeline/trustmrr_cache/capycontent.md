## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: For social media managers, creators, and founders who need consistent, on-brand Instagram carousels without a designer, CapyContent turns any idea into a ready-to-post carousel in about 2 minutes — instead of the hour it takes by hand in Canva or Figma.
- Problem solved: Posting consistent, on-brand Instagram carousels takes hours — write the copy, then rebuild the same layout by hand in Canva or Figma, slide by slide, every time — and most teams can't justify a designer just for that. CapyContent turns any idea into a ready-to-post, on-brand carousel in about 2 min
- Pricing model: $19.90/mo (Starter) to $39.90/mo (Growhth) plus a free trial with 120 credits, no credit card required.
- Target persona: Startup Founders
- Team size: 2–5 people
