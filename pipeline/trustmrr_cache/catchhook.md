## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Catchhook gives developers one place to capture, understand, test, replay, and route webhooks without piecing together request bins, tunnels, logs, and custom debugging tools.
- Problem solved: Webhook integrations are difficult to debug because requests are asynchronous, payloads are often complex, localhost is unreachable, and failures are scattered across provider dashboards and application logs. Catchhook makes the complete event lifecycle visible and reproducible.
