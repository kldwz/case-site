- Value proposition: Snap a photo of any meal. Carbsy estimates carbs, macros, and the glycemic impact — in seconds.

- Pricing model: Free · In-App Purchases · Weekly Plan $3.99, Annual Plan $39.99, Lifetime $39.99
- Target persona: Individuals managing PCOS, on a GLP-1, doing low-carb or keto diets, prediabetic individuals, or those curious about how meals affect their glucose and energy.
