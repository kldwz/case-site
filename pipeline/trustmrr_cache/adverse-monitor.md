## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Cyber threat intelligence and dark web monitoring that finds dark web incident claims naming your company or domain, with source evidence in one dashboard.
- Problem solved: Cuts the manual work of checking ransomware leak sites, Telegram, Tor, and open-web sources for company exposure.
- Pricing model: Free domain lookup and a 14-day trial with no credit card. Published annual plans: Detection $388, Protection $3,888, and Defend $20,000.
- Target persona: IT and security teams that need external exposure visibility.
- Audience type: [B2B](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2B)
- Team size: 1 person
- Funding: Bootstrapped
- Estimated user count: 50
- Additional info: Functional B2B cybersecurity SaaS with customer accounts, company and domain monitoring, searchable threat data, API access, and a free domain-check funnel. Built for annual subscriptions and supported by a public study of 29,858 threat records.
