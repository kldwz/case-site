## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Google Calendars, synchronized. One place blocks time on the others. Real-time. Privacy-preserving. Zero manual work after 90 seconds of setup.
- Problem solved: Solves the problem of managing multiple calendars by providing real-time synchronization and privacy-preserving 'Busy' blocks, preventing double-booking and ensuring all calendars are up-to-date.
- Pricing model: Lifetime: $9, Basic: $4/mo or $40/year, Pro: $10/mo or $100/year. Add calendars any time: $4/mo each.
- Target persona: People running more than one life, such as those with a day job and a side hustle, a career and a family, or an agency and a solo practice.
