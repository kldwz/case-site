## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Paste a job description and Applr rewrites your resume around that role's keywords in about 30 seconds, shows a live ATS match score, then lets you rehearse the interview for that exact job with an AI interviewer.
- Problem solved: International students send the same generic resume everywhere and get filtered out by ATS before a human reads it. They also rarely get to practice interviews in a second language. Applr fixes both in one place.
- Pricing model: Free: $0/mo (limited generations). Pro: $19.90/mo. Business: custom pricing for universities and career-coaching agencies.
- Target persona: International students and new grads job-hunting in the UK and US
- Audience type: [B2C](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2C)
- Team size: 1 person
- Funding: Bootstrapped
- Estimated user count: 100
- Additional info: Bilingual English/Chinese product. Resumes rendered as LaTeX PDFs. Free ATS resume checker on the landing page. Job feed aggregated from multiple boards. Solo-founder, bootstrapped, live since April 2026.
- Markets: [AI](https://trustmrr.com/category/ai), [Education](https://trustmrr.com/category/education), [Productivity](https://trustmrr.com/category/productivity), [Recruiting & HR](https://trustmrr.com/category/recruiting)
