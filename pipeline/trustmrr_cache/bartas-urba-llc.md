## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Studio-quality LinkedIn headshots from one selfie, ready in about a minute.
- Problem solved: Professional photoshoots are expensive and slow. Looksmith turns one selfie into hired-looking portraits for LinkedIn, resumes, and company sites.
- Pricing model: Pay once. Basic $29 (30 headshots, 1K), Professional $39 (50, 2K), Executive $59 (70, 4K). 1 free headshot per try, 5/day. 14-day refund.
- Target persona: Job seekers and professionals who need a LinkedIn or resume photo.
