## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Popular books key ideas summarized into 15 minutes for a bite sized, easy to digest, fun and time saving learning experience.
- Problem solved: 40% of people who buy books never actually start reading them, 60% don't make it past the first few chapters, and ~80% of purchased books are never completely read from start to finish.
- Pricing model: Free for 7 days. Try everything, it's on us
- Target persona: People with a busy lifestyle looking to gain new insights or make the most of their downtime.
