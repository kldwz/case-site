## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Convoclip is the free online chat story maker and text story maker that runs entirely in your browser — no app to download.
- Problem solved: Create fake text conversations, parkour texting stories, and AI quiz videos for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts in minutes without the need for any downloads.
- Pricing model: One-time (no subscription) $15 /(500 credits) Up to 16 videos, Auto-renew (monthly) $15 /(750 credits) Up to 25 videos
- Target persona: Creators looking to make engaging texting story videos for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts
