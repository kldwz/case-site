## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: AquaLock makes sure you actually drink water by locking the apps you waste time on and won't let you back in until you've logged your water intake, verified with a quick AI photo scan.
- Problem solved: Most hydration apps remind you to drink water. AquaLock ensures you actually drink it by tying hydration to screen time.
- Pricing model: Free · In‑App Purchases · AquaLock Pro Yearly $29.99 · AquaLock Pro Weekly $3.99
- Target persona: iPhone users who want to improve their hydration habits
