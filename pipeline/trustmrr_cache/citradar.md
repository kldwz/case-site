## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Enter your website and Citradar suggests the questions your buyers ask AI and your competitors. Every day, it checks ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity and shows your visibility score, your rank against competitors, and the sources AI cites.
