## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Cosmo lets Discord server owners sell directly in their server: digital products, subscriptions, physical goods, license keys, and gated roles, all with automated delivery. Built on Stripe Connect for reliable payouts, and fully Discord native, so buyers never leave the community to buy.
- Problem solved: Discord sellers stitch together bots, spreadsheets, manual role assignment, and separate payment links just to sell one product. It’s slow and error prone, and breaks trust when delivery is late. Cosmo automates payment, delivery, and access in one system built for how Discord servers work.
- Pricing model: $0/mo free forever, $9/mo Pro, $30/mo Business
- Target persona: Discord server owners
- Audience type: [B2C](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2C)
- Team size: 1 person
- Funding: Bootstrapped
- Estimated user count: 0
- Additional info: Cosmo is solo built and actively shipping, with real infrastructure: subscription handling, dispute management, multi currency support, security. Sellers range from small creators to high volume ones doing real recurring revenue. There’s also a partner program with 25% lifetime commission.
- Markets: [E-comm](https://trustmrr.com/category/ecommerce), [Marketplace](https://trustmrr.com/category/marketplace), [Community](https://trustmrr.com/category/community)
