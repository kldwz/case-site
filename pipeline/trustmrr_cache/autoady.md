## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Give agencies one place to review Meta ad performance, act on campaign recommendations, create ads and report to clients. AutoAdy connects the analysis to the next action, with automated rules and an AI assistant that can also be accessed through MCP.
- Problem solved: Managing several Meta ad accounts means repeated checks for wasted spend, creative fatigue and budget changes, followed by separate creative and reporting work. AutoAdy brings those tasks into one workflow so media buyers can spend less time moving between tools and more time reviewing decisions.
- Pricing model: Free: $0/month for 1 ad account. Pro: $69/month for up to 5 ad accounts. Agency: $149/month for up to 20 ad accounts, white-label reports and 10 team
- Target persona: Marketing agencies, freelance media buyers and in-house teams managing Facebook
- Audience type: [B2B](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2B)
- Team size: 1 person
- Funding: Bootstrapped
- Estimated user count: 15
- Additional info: AutoAdy is a live, pre-revenue software acquisition for a buyer who already reaches advertisers. An agency could use it across client accounts and test a paid software offer with its existing customer base. A marketing SaaS operator could add Meta ads management to its product offering.
