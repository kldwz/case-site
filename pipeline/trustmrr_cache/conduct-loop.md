## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: ConductLoop turns AI agents from isolated chat sessions into persistent teams that can remember context, share knowledge and work across projects. Users retain control over their models and data while avoiding vendor lock-in. It provides a single workspace for orchestrating multiple AI agents.
- Problem solved: AI agents often lose context between sessions, operate in isolation and require users to repeatedly provide the same information. Managing multiple agents across different models and projects also creates fragmented workflows. ConductLoop solves this by providing persistent memory, shared knowledge.
- Pricing model: Free forever — with optional paid cloud features planned for AI hosting, synchronization and team workspaces.
- Target persona: AI power users, developers, founders, solo entrepreneurs, agencies and smalll te
- Audience type: [Both](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=Both)
- Team size: 1 person
- Funding: Bootstrapped
- Estimated user count: 10
- Additional info: ConductLoop is designed as a local-first and model-agnostic platform. Users can bring their own AI providers or run models locally, giving them greater control over their data and infrastructure. The desktop application is already live, with the product designed to expand into cloud synchronization,
