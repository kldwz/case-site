## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Practice the sales calls and salary negotiations that decide your income, out loud, against an AI counterpart that pushes back. Then get a scored review of exactly where you lost the room.
- Problem solved: Reps rehearse the pitch and never rehearse the pushback, so their first attempt at the hardest moment of a call is a live prospect with real money on the line.
- Pricing model: Free (600 credits/month), Pro $79/month, Max $149/month
- Target persona: SDRs, account executives, founders selling their own product, and candidates pre
- Audience type: [B2C](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2C)
- Team size: 1 person
- Funding: Bootstrapped
- Estimated user count: 0
- Additional info: Real-time voice, not chat. Holds a sub-second response budget in production, so it feels like a call rather than a chatbot. Covers cold calls, discovery, pitching, objection handling and salary negotiation, and scores every call against a rubric.
- Markets: [AI](https://trustmrr.com/category/ai), [Sales](https://trustmrr.com/category/sales), [Education](https://trustmrr.com/category/education)
