## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Upload a photo, choose one of 20 style presets — from Scandinavian minimalist to Cyberpunk — and receive a designer-quality render in under 60 seconds. No 3D modeling software, no CAD files, no designer required.
- Problem solved: Transforms photos of real rooms and building facades into photorealistic redesigns without the need for software or hiring a designer.
- Pricing model: $9.99 per week for 30 credits
- Target persona: Homeowners planning renovations, renters visualizing non-permanent changes, real estate agents creating virtual staging, and interior designers generating early-stage client concepts.
- Audience type: [B2C](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2C)
