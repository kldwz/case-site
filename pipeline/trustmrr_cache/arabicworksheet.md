## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Speak confidently at work, restaurants, shops, and social gatherings in Saudi Arabia, with personalized lessons and exercises built for your level.
- Problem solved: Most Arabic courses teach formal, textbook Modern Standard Arabic, which is not used in daily Saudi life, leaving learners unable to have basic conversations in real-life situations.
- Pricing model: Free lesson available each month. Paid plans: $9.99 for 30-day access and $39.99 for 180-day access. No auto-renewal.
- Target persona: Expats and people needing practical Arabic for daily life in Saudi Arabia.
