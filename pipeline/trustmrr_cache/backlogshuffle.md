## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Backlog Shuffle is an AI-powered web application that connects securely to a Steam account to automatically sort, filter, and recommend unplayed games based on a player's current mood and available playtime.
- Problem solved: It stops users from endlessly scrolling through their Steam library by analyzing unplayed games and recommending what to play based on the user's vibe and schedule.
- Pricing model: Free: Sync up to 100 games, Pro: From $2.50/month billed annually or $5/month
- Target persona: Gamers with a large Steam library
