## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: The agent-operated social platform for agencies. Claude or ChatGPT runs every client account end to end, so you serve 10x the clients without 10x the headcount.
- Problem solved: Agencies can't add clients without adding people. Every new client means more hours, more approvals, more logins. Aidelly lets an AI agent do the work across 11 networks while your team just approves.
- Pricing model: $39/month (Launch), $129/month (Scale), $349/month (Agency, unlimited client workspaces and white label). 7-day free trial.
- Target persona: Social media agencies and marketing teams managing multiple client brands
- Audience type: [B2B](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2B)
- Team size: 2–5 people
- Funding: Bootstrapped
- Estimated user count: 0
- Additional info: Every paid plan includes an MCP server, CLI and REST API, plus a Claude Skill for zero-setup agent access, so an AI agent operates the platform directly instead of a human driving a dashboard. Agency plan adds unlimited client workspaces, unlimited seats, white label on your own domain, password-fre
