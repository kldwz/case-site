## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Instantly blur sensitive data on your screen while recording or screen sharing - no post-editing needed.
- Problem solved: Blurring sensitive info in post-production is slow, error-prone, and impossible for live calls. BlurScreen hides it the moment it appears on screen.
- Pricing model: Basic: $19 one-time, Professional: $47 one-time, Team: $97 one-time
