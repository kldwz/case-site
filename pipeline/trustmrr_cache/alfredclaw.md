## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: An AI Agent that lives on WhatsApp or Telegram, remembers everything, manages your calendar, browses the web, and gets smarter over time.
- Problem solved: ChatGPT in WhatsApp has no memory between conversations, cannot access your calendar/email, and Meta processes your messages. AlfredClaw remembers your context, connects to your tools, and runs in an isolated container.
- Pricing model: 1 Agent: €14.90/mo, 5 Agents: €49/mo, Custom Setup: Let's Talk
- Target persona: Personal users, teams & families
