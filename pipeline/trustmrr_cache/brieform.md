## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Say a sentence in your AI chat, get a live form URL back, and read the responses in the same thread.
- Problem solved: Building a form today means leaving your AI chat to retype every field in a form builder, then copy-pasting responses back in for analysis.
- Pricing model: Free $0/mo (1 form, 50 responses/mo), Starter $29/mo (20 forms, 2,000 responses/mo, no branding), Pro $69/mo (unlimited , priority support)
- Target persona: Small teams and solo builders who already work in Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or an
- Audience type: [Both](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=Both)
- Team size: 1 person
- Funding: Bootstrapped
- Estimated user count: 18
- Additional info: AI-native form builder with a full MCP server on every plan: 12 MCP tools, 13 field types, multi-step forms, conditional logic, CSV export, EU hosting (Frankfurt), no credit card to start.
