## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Keep comment review, reply drafting, team handoff, and reporting in one workspace. Work with the original video or post context, use AI to prepare drafts and group feedback, and assign follow-ups without losing the conversation.
- Problem solved: Teams managing comments across accounts can miss questions, lose track of ownership, and spend time assembling client reports. Comment Command keeps conversations, labels, internal notes, reply drafts, and exports together so the team can review and follow up consistently.
- Pricing model: Subscriptions: Starter $12/mo, Pro $39/mo, Agency $149/mo. Annual billing available. X is an optional add-on; plan limits apply.
- Target persona: Social media agencies, brand teams, community managers, and creators.
- Audience type: [B2B](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2B)
- Team size: 1 person
- Estimated user count: 0
- Additional info: Live web application built and maintained by a solo developer. Public walkthroughs use synthetic demo data. Platform features depend on connected-account eligibility, permissions, and plan limits. AI reply drafts are reviewed by the operator before use.
