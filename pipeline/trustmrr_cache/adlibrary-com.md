## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: AdLibrary.com is the most comprehensive ad search engine on the internet, indexing over 1 billion advertisements across Meta, Google, YouTube, Tiktok, Twitter and Native mobile ad networks.
- Problem solved: Unified search across all major ad platforms to avoid the hassle of checking multiple tabs and platforms individually.
- Pricing model: AdLibrary costs $179/month, but only 29$ for the first month to explore all features. Free Forever Option: Pay as you go plan with some bonus credits.
- Target persona: Performance marketers, media buyers, creative strategists, and growth teams at companies spending $100k-10M+ monthly on paid advertising.
