## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: An AI that actually knows you and holds you accountable: it keeps your goals in memory, checks in when you slip, and turns conversations into widgets you can act on.
- Problem solved: Generic AI assistants forget everything between chats and never follow up. People set goals, lose momentum in two weeks, and nobody notices.
- Pricing model: One plan, no free trial: €29.99/month or €299/year. Credit top-up at €4.99 when the monthly AI budget is used up.
- Target persona: Consumers who want a personal coach for habits, fitness, nutrition and productiv
