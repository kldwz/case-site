## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Connect your AI agent to the data it can't reach. From live SEO and ranking data to your bookmarks and competitor moves, all inside Claude and Cursor
- Problem solved: Gives AI agents the context they can't reach, so you skip the SEO dashboards and tab-switching
- Pricing model: SEO $35/mo, Radar $39/mo, Bookmarks free (up to 150 saves) then $6/mo
- Target persona: Founders, marketers and developers who use AI agents like Claude and Cursor
- Audience type: [B2B](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2B)
- Funding: Bootstrapped
- Estimated user count: 0
- Additional info: MCP-native, local-first, no lock-in. Also includes Bookmarks (query your saved X/Reddit/LinkedIn posts by meaning) and Radar (nightly competitor monitoring). Works in Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Cursor, Windsurf, Cline, Zed and Codex.
