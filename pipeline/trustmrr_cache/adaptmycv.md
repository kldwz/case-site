## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: AdaptMyCV tailors your CV to the exact job description in under a minute — matching the keywords ATS filters score against, grounded in your real experience, with an honesty check that blocks fabricated claims.

- Pricing model: Pay per CV, no subscription. Building a CV is free, restyling a PDF is 3 coins, and AI CV adaptation is 10 coins (5 coins = $1).
